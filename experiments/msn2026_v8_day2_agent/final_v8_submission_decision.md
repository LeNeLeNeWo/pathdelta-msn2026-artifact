# Final v8 submission decision

## Decision: NO-GO for an MSN 2026 submission on the current evidence

The research direction remains worth pursuing: “which existing behavior is permitted to change?” is a cleaner Day-2 abstraction than asking an LLM to beat a symbolic compiler, and the system maintains genuine model-owned editing. But a submission now would rely on a perfect, co-designed development classification result while lacking the decisive same-LLM feedback effect.

The formal protocol was not admitted. Specifically, independent labels/public candidate outcomes are missing; the paired RQ2 pilot produced no difference; preserve-all and dependency-blacklist alternatives have not been ruled out; Batfish/Rela covers one scenario; and no new Kathara dynamic sample exists. These are core novelty/evidence gaps, not polish.

This NO-GO means “do not submit or write results as if formal,” not “change topics.” The next legitimate milestone is the preregistered admission package in `formal_go_no_go_protocol.md`. If it passes without changing labels or resampling until favorable, the thesis may become submission-ready.
