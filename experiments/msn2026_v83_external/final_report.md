# PathDelta MSN 2026 v8.3 experiment report

## Executive conclusion

The enlarged study supports a narrower and more credible paper than the prior
``Full Envelope is perfect'' story.  A patch-independent Change Envelope is a
useful semantic authorization boundary: goal-only and write-scope checks accept
all 120 target-achieving collateral patches in the registered external model,
whereas Full accepts none and rejects none of 58 registered-safe alternatives.
The difference from the strong visible-test VerifierLoop is 12/120 candidates
(10 percentage points, exact McNemar p=0.000488; Holm-adjusted p=0.00293).
This misses the preregistered 15-point superiority gate, so the external
GO/NO-GO decision is **NO-GO** and the mechanism was not retuned.

More importantly, an independent Batfish symbolic audit found a limitation in
the registered finite attribute oracle.  On 162 goal-achieving candidates for
which both models are applicable, the lightweight Full boundary misses 12
Batfish-visible metric/origin-type regressions, while Batfish misses 10
collateral cases covered by the registered behavior witnesses.  A fail-closed
union rejects all 119 cross-model unsafe candidates and accepts all 43
cross-model safe candidates.  This union is an exploratory, post-unblinding
hardening result, not a confirmatory endpoint.  It changes the strongest paper
claim from ``one inferred model proves safety'' to ``explicit intent-relative
obligations let heterogeneous evidence expose and close non-overlapping model
gaps.''

Agent128 confirms the safety/usability tension.  Full releases no registered
collateral and repairs 16 rejected first candidates to safe completion, but
only completes 49/128 events and exhausts the five-submission budget in 79.
Visible VerifierLoop completes 40/128 and releases five collateral changes.
Thus the experiment supports safer release, not universal task completion or
LLM superiority.

## E1: registered external boundary challenge

The frozen candidate pool contains 178 target-achieving candidates: 58 labeled
safe and 120 labeled unsafe under the registered dimensions (decision,
local-pref, communities, and session).  The remaining 75 do not achieve the
target and are excluded from FA/FR denominators, not deleted from the corpus.

| Boundary | Unsafe accepted | Unsafe FA | Safe rejected | Safe FR |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Goal-only | 120/120 | 100% | 0/58 | 0% |
| Write-scope | 120/120 | 100% | 0/58 | 0% |
| Visible+Scope | 12/120 | 10% | 0/58 | 0% |
| Visible VerifierLoop | 12/120 | 10% | 0/58 | 0% |
| OracleContract | 0/120 | 0% | 0/58 | 0% |
| Registered Full | 0/120 | 0% | 0/58 | 0% |

Full's Wilson 95% upper bounds are 3.10% for unsafe FA and 6.21% for safe FR.
Full matches the registered manual OracleContract.  The direction holds in
both source groups and is strict for Cisco and Arista; Junos has no
VerifierLoop/Full discordance under the registered dimensions.

Leave-one-out results identify the operative components.  Removing the
semantic frame accepts all 120 unsafe candidates.  Removing active witnesses
accepts 12.  Removing dependency protection or footprint alone changes no
verdict in this external pool; those components remain non-decisive here and
must not be claimed essential from this experiment.

## E2: missing-coverage sensitivity

Twenty replicates were run at 10%, 25%, and 50% passive-record removal under
uniform-random and adversarial removal.  The mean unsafe FA rates are:

| Removal | Random passive | Random + active | Adversarial passive | Adversarial + active |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 10% | 12.67% | 0.88% | 25.0% | 4.17% |
| 25% | 15.54% | 2.13% | 30.0% | 5.0% |
| 50% | 20.71% | 2.29% | 82.5% | 5.0% |

Active-witness safe FR is 0 in every condition.  These are finite recovery
rates, not a coverage theorem; silent classes outside both passive and active
models remain a threat.

## E3: backend applicability and independent symbolic audit

Batfish parses 234/253 candidates; 19 are N/A, of which 15 inherit a
partially-recognized baseline and four are candidate-induced.  There are no
backend errors.  A hard Batfish parse gate would reject three of 58
registered-safe goal candidates (5.17% additional FR).  The vendor adapter is
applicable to all 253, so every candidate has at least one finite-evidence
backend; this does not imply full semantic coverage.

The wider independent `searchRoutePolicies`/`compareRoutePolicies` audit is
applicable to 233/253 candidates and materializes 557 symbolic equivalence
classes.  Among registered goal candidates it covers 55 registered-safe and
107 registered-unsafe cases:

- it passes 43/55 registered-safe candidates and exposes 12 as changing
  `metric` and `originType` in addition to the authorized local preference;
- it rejects 97/107 registered-unsafe candidates and misses ten whose
  registered collateral falls outside the returned Batfish witness relation;
- 20/253 remain symbolic N/A.

Using the union label on the 162 jointly applicable goal candidates gives 43
safe and 119 unsafe.  Registered Full alone accepts 12/119 unsafe; Batfish
alone accepts 10/119; fail-closed union accepts 0/119 unsafe and 43/43 safe.
This analysis was designed after the independent audit exposed a dimension
gap and is therefore explicitly exploratory.

## E4: paired Agent128

The 128 case IDs were selected by a label-independent seeded shuffle and then
frozen.  Post-freeze composition is 33 safe-goal, 56 unsafe-goal, and 39
non-goal first candidates across Cornetto/Batfish and Cisco/Arista/Junos.  All
causal arms replay the identical first patch; RAG changes the prompt and is
reported as matched but non-causal.  Each iterative method receives only
patch-free violated relations and may submit at most five baseline-relative
patches.

| Method | Verified completion | Collateral released | Failed intent released | Reject→safe repair | Exhausted |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Direct replay | 33/128 | 56 | 39 | 0 | 0 |
| RAG one-shot | 16/128 | 48 | 63 | 0 | 0 |
| Goal-only | 43/128 | 61 | 0 | 10 | 24 |
| Write-scope | 39/128 | 59 | 0 | 6 | 30 |
| Visible VerifierLoop | 40/128 | 5 | 0 | 7 | 83 |
| OracleContract | 45/128 | 0 | 0 | 12 | 83 |
| Registered Full | 49/128 | 0 | 0 | 16 | 79 |

The difference in completion between Full and OracleContract is not attributed
to a superior boundary: repair calls are separate model samples after the
shared first candidate.  Confirmatory causal interpretation is restricted to
first-candidate decisions; revision outcomes show operational behavior.

Full's 79 failures are 47 semantic-collateral fail-closed cases and 32 target
unmet cases, all at attempt exhaustion.  This makes the limitation concrete:
counterexample feedback often identifies the violation but does not reliably
lead this model to a safe implementation within five attempts.

Agent128 uses 1,416 logical calls and 1,416 backend attempts, with zero
transport retries.  Usage is 6,077,018 prompt + 322,519 completion = 6,399,537
tokens.  Aggregate API latency is 3,270.97 seconds; per-call P50/P95 are
2.07/3.55 seconds.  The Full branch averages 2.68 revision calls and 12,017
tokens per event, excluding the independently accounted first-candidate
generation.  Red-team generation adds 96 calls and 422,843 tokens.

## E5: scalability

Fresh synthetic scale inputs use seed `20260814` and separate hashes.  Envelope
compilation plus materialization takes 9.81 s at 100k FECs (100 objects), 25.83
s at 500 devices/100k FECs, and 90.92 s at the coupled 10k-object/100k-FEC
point.  Peak measured Python memory is 42.63 MiB.  Batfish parse samples pass at
0.40 s for 100--1,000 objects, 1.49 s for 10k objects, and 1.02 s for 100
devices.  The 90.92 s coupled point is the current implementation's principal
scalability limitation.

## Claim disposition for the paper

The paper should claim:

1. Goal and write-scope authorization are insufficient for Day-2 semantic
   collateral, including on public-source, independently generated candidates.
2. Patch-independent target/frame obligations constrain an LLM without
   prescribing a reference patch; active witnesses recover many missing
   passive classes.
3. Every finite semantic model has coverage boundaries.  Explicit obligation
   provenance enables heterogeneous evidence to expose non-overlapping gaps;
   fail-closed union is safer than either registered adapter or Batfish alone
   on the jointly applicable audit subset.
4. In Agent128, stronger boundaries eliminate registered unsafe release and
   sometimes guide repair, but the price is substantial fail-closed attempt
   exhaustion.  The work is a safety boundary, not an LLM success-rate claim.

The paper must not claim universal safety, Full superiority over OracleContract,
that every Envelope component is independently necessary, or that the
exploratory cross-backend union was preregistered.
